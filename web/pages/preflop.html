Game

Raises are multiples of the previous bet. A raise above the all-in threshold becomes all-in. A hand that reaches the flop is settled by all-in equity among the players left, so postflop play is not modelled; with three players in, the opponents' cards are treated as independent given the hero's hand. With two players the solution is an equilibrium of this game; with more players DCFR has no convergence guarantee and the page reports the measured NashConv.
